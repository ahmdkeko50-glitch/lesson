# Grade 6 Science (العلوم — السادس الابتدائي): Interactive Activity Analysis

**Source:** the book's web export (`interactive/`, 212 pages, every scene read).
Facts used in the activities come only from that text. Where an activity needs something the book does not state, the page labels it as "an illustration, not from the book".

## 0. What the book already has, and the gap

The web book is already interactive. Nearly every scene has:
- drag-to-label tasks
- drag-to-sort and fill-the-blank tasks
- multiple-choice and true/false questions
- flip vocabulary cards
- typed answers checked by keywords
- a few fixed animations (wind and water sliders, a day slider, a circuit switch)

**Three gaps repeat in every chapter:**
1. **No real simulation.** No experiment lets a choice change the outcome: no variables, no cause and effect, no "what if I skip this step?".
2. **No spatial exploration.** Layered or internal structures (seed, skin, atom, skeleton joints) appear only as flat images with label slots.
3. **Unanswered questions.** Many "أفكّر وأجيب" / "التفكير الناقد" / "أستنتج" questions have no answer key, so students get no feedback. A simulation can let students *observe* the answer instead.

The new activities target these gaps. None of them repeats an existing label drag.

**Chapter 7 is incomplete in the export.** Its two lessons (Mass & Weight, The Lever) are missing; only the science reading and the chapter review exist. It is not used as a source here.

**Why these 8.** Each covers a different lesson, and together they span 5 of the 7 chapters with source content. Each was ranked on:
- the size of the gap left by the existing scenes
- how abstract, invisible or spatial the concept is
- whether the student's own choices can produce feedback that explains *why*

**Close but not chosen:**
- **Eye/ear pathways (24, 25):** strong, but the book never states the pathways as ordered steps, so an activity would have to infer the order.
- **Grafting (12):** a good procedure, but the existing scenes already order the steps.
- **Banana stem (16):** narrow scope.
- **Tide (4):** mostly animation with little decision-making.

## 3. Activity specifications

### 1. أبني ذرّتي (Build my atom)

- **Book location:** Chapter 5 «بناء المادة», Lesson 5-1 «الذرة» (activity-book pp. 37–39). Also uses chapter review Q9 and Q13.
- **Learning objective:** list the parts of the atom; state where each part sits, its charge and its relative mass; explain why an atom is neutral.
- **Why interactivity helps:**
  - The atom is invisible, and the book shows it only as fixed pictures.
  - The questions "لو فقدت الذرة إلكتروناً؟" (what if the atom loses an electron?) and "2 أم 8 إلكترونات أكبر؟" (is an atom with 2 or 8 electrons bigger?) have no answer key.
  - Here the student changes the counts and *sees* the charge and size respond.
- **Type:** 3D construction (rotatable model on a canvas) + simulation.
- **Student action:**
  - Add or remove protons, neutrons and electrons.
  - Rotate the 3D model.
  - Complete missions: build helium (2p, 2n, 2e) and hydrogen (1p, 1e); compare lithium (3e) with hydrogen; remove an electron and predict the charge.
- **Expected learning:**
  - The nucleus is at the centre and holds protons (+) and neutrons (no charge).
  - Electrons (−) orbit the nucleus.
  - The atom is neutral when electrons = protons.
  - Mass is almost all in the nucleus.
  - More electrons → a larger atom.
- **Feedback:**
  - A live charge meter and mass bar.
  - A neutral / positive / negative badge.
  - Each mission is checked with an explanation, e.g. "عدد البروتونات ٢ والإلكترونات ١، الشحنة الموجبة أكثر…" (2 protons and 1 electron, so there is more positive charge).
- **Age fit:** concrete counting and building turns an abstract idea into a toy-like model, while the reasoning stays at the book's level.

### 2. مركز القيادة (The brain at work)

- **Book location:** Chapter 3, Lesson 3-1 «الجهاز العصبي وصحته» (activity-book pp. 20–22). Also uses chapter review Q14 and Q16.
- **Learning objective:**
  - Name the CNS parts (brain: المخ / المخيخ / النخاع المستطيل; الحبل الشوكي) and the peripheral nerves.
  - Match each brain part to its function.
  - Describe how orders travel as electrical signals along the nerves.
- **Why interactivity helps:**
  - The goalkeeper picture-reading question and review Q16 have no answer key.
  - The function of each brain part is easiest to learn from situations.
  - Cutting a nerve makes the job of the nerves visible.
- **Type:** interactive diagram + simulation.
- **Student action:**
  1. Tap the parts of the brain and spinal cord.
  2. "Which part is working?": sort 9 everyday situations to المخ / المخيخ / النخاع المستطيل.
  3. Goalkeeper: send the signal step by step along the path and watch the electrical pulse travel. Cut a nerve and see the hand not move.
- **Expected learning:**
  - المخ: memory, thinking, information from the senses.
  - المخيخ: balance.
  - النخاع المستطيل: heartbeat, breathing, blood pressure; it connects to the spinal cord.
  - Nerves carry information to and from the brain as electrical signals.
- **Feedback:** each sort is explained with the book's function sentence. The impulse path is checked step by step with a hint. The cut-nerve outcome is explained with the book's circuit analogy.
- **Age fit:** sports and everyday scenarios; short steps.

### 3. داخل البذرة (Inside the seed)

- **Book location:** Chapter 1, Lesson 1-1 «التكاثر بالبذور» (activity-book pp. 5–8).
- **Learning objective:**
  - Name the seed parts and their functions.
  - Tell one-cotyledon seeds (corn) from two-cotyledon seeds (beans).
  - Explain that germination needs suitable soil, enough water and nutrients.
- **Why interactivity helps:**
  - Internal structure is hidden: the coat must be peeled and the seed split.
  - The existing day slider is a fixed animation with no variables.
  - The question "هل البذور الجافة ميتة؟" (are dry seeds dead?) has no feedback.
- **Type:** exploration (peel and split, 2.5D) + simulation (growth factors).
- **Student action:**
  1. Peel the bean's coat, split it into its two cotyledons, and tap the parts. Then cut the corn seed.
  2. Sort the book's seeds by number of cotyledons.
  3. Germination chamber: switch soil, water and nutrients on or off, predict, run 5 days, and observe. A dry seed waits; once water is added it swells, the radicle grows down and the plumule grows up.
- **Expected learning:**
  - The parts: coat, cotyledons, embryo (radicle → root, plumule → stem), endosperm.
  - Germination stages and their factors.
- **Feedback:** each part shows its function. Sorting is explained. The germination result explains which factor was missing.
- **Age fit:** a hands-on peel-and-split model with a clear prediction-then-observation loop.

### 4. مختبر الجزيئات (Molecule lab)

- **Book location:** Chapter 5, Lesson 5-2 «العناصر والمركبات» (activity-book pp. 40–42). Also uses review Q14 and the methane performance task.
- **Learning objective:**
  - A molecule is atoms joined by chemical bonds.
  - Similar atoms make an **element molecule**; different atoms make a **compound molecule**.
  - Bonds break and form in a chemical change.
- **Why interactivity helps:** the existing builds use fixed slots for one answer, and "أستكشف أكثر: ابنِ جزيئات أخرى" (explore more: build other molecules) has no tool.
- **Type:** 3D construction (ball-and-stick, rotatable) + classification.
- **Student action:**
  - Free build: pick atoms (hydrogen, oxygen, carbon, chlorine) and join them with bonds.
  - The lab names the molecule if it is one of the book's examples and classifies it automatically.
  - Missions: H₂, O₂, water, CO₂, HCl, methane.
  - Tear a bond (review Q14).
- **Expected learning:** the element/compound distinction and the bond as the "cement".
- **Feedback:** a live classification with its reason ("نوع واحد من الذرات ← جزيء عنصر", one kind of atom → element molecule). Each mission checks the atom counts and bonds and explains any mismatch.
- **Age fit:** clay-model building in 3D, using only colour names and Arabic names. There are no chemical symbols, because the book uses none.

### 5. مختبر التغيّر (Change lab)

- **Book location:** Chapter 6, Lesson 6-1 «مفهوم التفاعل الكيميائي» (activity-book pp. 43–45), plus the Lesson 6-2 word equation and the review performance task (steel wool).
- **Learning objective:**
  - Tell a physical change (no new substance) from a chemical reaction (new substances).
  - Identify reactants and products.
  - Write the word equation for heating sugar.
- **Why interactivity helps:**
  - The real experiments need a flame, electricity or days of waiting.
  - The step-4 and step-7 predictions in the sugar experiment have no feedback.
  - Students confuse melting with reacting.
- **Type:** virtual lab.
- **Student action:** four stations.
  1. Sugar in tea: stir it.
  2. Heating sugar: gentle heat → melts and vapour; inverted cup → droplets; keep heating → black residue.
  3. Water electrolysis: connect the battery → bubbles at both leads; the water decreases over time.
  4. Steel wool, wet vs dry: a days slider.

  At each station the student answers "هل تكوّنت مادة جديدة؟" (did a new substance form?) and picks the evidence. Finally they build the equation سكر —حرارة→ ماء + كربون (sugar → heat → water + carbon).
- **Expected learning:** the new-substance test; the book's examples; decomposition; reactants and products.
- **Feedback:** each classification is explained with the book's definitions. The misconception "melting = reaction" is addressed directly at the melting moment.
- **Age fit:** safe access to experiments that are dangerous or slow; a clear yes/no test.

### 6. رحلة داخل الجلد (Inside the skin)

- **Book location:** Chapter 4, Lesson 4-2 «تركيب الجلد ووظائفه» (activity-book pp. 33–36).
- **Learning objective:**
  - Name the two skin layers and what each contains.
  - Explain how sweating regulates temperature and is a form of excretion.
  - Explain the sense of touch through nerve endings.
- **Why interactivity helps:** the existing page is a flat image. The idea that the glands, vessels and nerve endings lie *inside* the dermis is spatial.
- **Type:** 3D exploration (rotatable block, separate the layers) + simulation.
- **Student action:**
  - Rotate the 3D skin block and lift off the epidermis.
  - Tap structures, then place each one in "البشرة" (epidermis) or "الأدمة" (dermis).
  - Run the hot-day sim: raise the temperature → sweat glands secrete → sweat leaves through the pores → body temperature drops.
  - Touch sim: a touch reaches a nerve ending and the message goes to the brain.
- **Expected learning:**
  - Epidermis: the outer layer; keeps fluids in; has pores.
  - Dermis: blood vessels, nerve endings, sebaceous glands, hair, sweat glands.
  - The skin's functions.
- **Feedback:** layer placement is explained, and the sweating loop is shown on a thermometer.
- **Age fit:** a tangible 3D model with short labels.

### 7. كيف أتحرّك؟ (How do I move?)

- **Book location:**
  - Chapter 3, Lesson 3-2 «الجهاز الهيكلي» (joints, cartilage).
  - Lesson 3-3 «الجهاز العضلي» (the explore model, activity-book p.27–28; contraction and relaxation; voluntary and involuntary).
- **Learning objective:**
  - Joints connect bones and allow movement: ball (shoulder), cylindrical (forearm), fixed (skull).
  - Cartilage prevents friction.
  - Muscles move bones by contracting and relaxing.
  - Tendons attach muscles to bone.
  - The motor system = bones + muscles.
- **Why interactivity helps:**
  - The joint types are only a 3-card text sort today.
  - The rubber-band model needs wood, nails and hammers.
  - The second-band extension has no conclusion.
- **Type:** 3D manipulation + simulation.
- **Student action:**
  1. Joint lab: try to move the shoulder in all directions, the forearm joint (one direction) and the skull (no movement). Remove the cartilage → a friction warning.
  2. Muscle model: bend the book's model with a slider; the band shortens or lengthens; add the second band; map the strips to bones, the hinge to a joint and the band to a muscle.
  3. Voluntary vs involuntary: command the arm (it moves) vs the heart (it keeps beating by itself).
- **Expected learning:** joint types and movement; contraction bends the arm; a muscle pulls a bone via the joint; voluntary vs involuntary.
- **Feedback:** each trial is explained with the book's definitions.
- **Age fit:** students can test the model with their own arm as they go.

### 8. مزرعة الدرنات (Tuber farm)

- **Book location:** Chapter 1, Lesson 1-2 «التكاثر الخضري» (activity-book pp. 9–12). Also uses review Q14 and Q18.
- **Learning objective:**
  - A tuber is a modified underground stem with eyes.
  - Large tubers are cut so that each piece has two groups of eyes.
  - Pieces are left until a cork layer forms, which protects against fungal disease and rot.
  - Vegetative reproduction is faster than seeds.
- **Why interactivity helps:** the procedure is a set of decisions with consequences. The existing scenes give only text and fixed slider frames.
- **Type:** decision simulation.
- **Student action:** draw cut lines on a virtual potato; the eye groups in each piece are counted. Decide whether to wait for the cork layer. Plant the pieces, fast-forward and see which pieces sprout or rot, then fix the plan.
- **Expected learning:** the cutting rules and their reasons; classifying tubers vs bulbs with the book's examples.
- **Feedback:** each piece's outcome comes with its reason ("هذه القطعة فيها مجموعة عيون واحدة فقط", this piece has only one group of eyes).
- **Age fit:** a farming mini-game; fast and forgiving.

## 4. Shared UX/UI structure (all activities)

- **Single file, offline:**
  - no CDN, no web fonts (system Arabic font stack), RTL Arabic
  - 3D is drawn on a hand-written canvas projection or CSS 3D, with no libraries
- **Layout:**
  - **Header:** book reference chip (chapter · lesson · activity-book pages), activity title, stage progress dots.
  - **Main:** one stage at a time.
  - **Footer:** back and next buttons.
- **Learning flow:** each activity runs Explore → Predict → Manipulate/Experiment → Observe → Apply (challenge) → summary card.
- **Feedback:** an inline panel that always says *why* (role=status, aria-live). It is never just "wrong": it says "حاول مرة أخرى…" (try again…) with a reason, and offers "أرني الحل" (show me the solution) after 2 tries.
- **Visual:**
  - The book's palette: navy `#200058`, cyan `#00c3fe`/`#0aa3d6`, green `#12a06a`, red `#e8425a`, yellow `#ffd15c`; light lavender background.
  - Rounded cards; 48px+ touch targets.
  - Icons plus text, so meaning never depends on colour alone.
- **Input:** pointer events (mouse and touch), tap-to-select-then-tap-target as a drag alternative, keyboard focus and Enter/Space.
- **Motion:** short transitions; `prefers-reduced-motion` turns animation off; any celebration is small.
